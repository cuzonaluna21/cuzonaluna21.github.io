🌟 What is DuoFold-Android?

DuoFold-Android is a system-wide fold illusion for your regular Android phone. It simulates the experience of a foldable dual-screen device — just like the iPhone Duo — but without any special hardware. The whole screen gets reprojected in real time based on how you physically move your phone in space.

Hold your phone flat, tilt it slightly, pivot it around — and watch the screen respond with a beautiful 3D folding effect. It feels like you are looking at a real foldable device, but you are just using your everyday phone candidate!

Every part of the system interface gets included in this folding view — that's what makes it system-wide. It's not just a single wallpaper or app; your entire screen transforms as you move.

🔧 How It Works (Simple Words)

You probably have a normal phone. Your phone has tiny sensors inside that know exactly how you're holding it. DuoFold-Android reads those sensors thousands of times every second.

At the same time, it takes what's currently shown on your screen and wraps it around an imaginary fold line — almost like paper folding. As you physically rotate your phone, the fold appears to open or close in real life. The result is that your screen looks like it's actually bending in the middle, even thought your hardware is perfectly flat.

Because it works at the system level, every app participates in this illusion. It's a complete transformation of your device.
